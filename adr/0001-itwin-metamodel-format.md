## Context

DESIGN.md v0.1.0 section 7 defers the choice of formal metamodel for an iTwin. Candidates: JSON Schema, RDF/Turtle with SHACL (natural fit for WSF grounding), Pydantic models, or a layered combination.

## Decision

Pending. Evaluation criteria: semantic fidelity, machine-parseability for tooling, readability for digital architects and domain experts, WSF/OpenDEAM grounding cost.

## Consequences

To be recorded with the decision.
